# Assembling your macropad!

Finally got your parts? Awesome! Here's what you need to do.

First of all, congratulate yourself for getting your parts! It's probably surreal to get your hands on these.

I'd suggest watching some basic THT soldering video's on YouTube before getting started and maybe even doing a bit of practice soldering with extra components so you don't mess up on the real thing. 

Most soldering mistakes are fixable with the proper tools, and sometimes people will think that they've fried a component, but in reality, it's sometimes just badly soldered and needs to be touched up, so keep on going with it :D

For soldering the XIAO, I'd suggest inserting it into a breadboard with the header pins in, and then soldering it so that the header pins are aligned nicely.

### 1. Solder your diodes!

Note the direction of the diodes

Make sure to clip the legs off after - it'll be a lot easier.

### 2. Solder your other components!

Constantly check against your CAD to make sure everything is right

### 3. Clip your switches into your plate!

It's important that before you just solder the switches into the PCB, that you clip the switches into the top plate, and then do it, because you won't be able to slide the top plate over it otherwise.
